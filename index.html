I read the guidelines from the two pages you attached. The tracking spreadsheet and the FAO template are still missing, so the FAO report stays a draft placeholder. No policy is invented: anything not in these documents is marked as such.

Facts taken from the application form

  • Subsidy "up to P30,000" for international travel and "up to P15,000" for local travel, subject to availability of funds.
  • Available once every academic year, after considering all other funding sources.
  • Criteria: DLSU-CCS undergraduate (within one year of completion if the paper comes from a thesis); paper in a Scopus, ISI or ASEAN Citation listed proceedings; endorsement from the Associate Dean and Thesis Adviser.
  • Priority to first-time applicants; one student co-author funded per paper; one paper per faculty per conference.
  • Expense rows: registration, pre-departure fees, roundtrip airfare, accommodation, per diem, each with amount, funding source and remarks.
  • A conference report and liquidation with receipts are required after the activity.

Facts taken from the guidelines

  • Scope is wider than conferences: competitions, exhibits and public fora too. Funding is partial and discretionary.
  • Eligibility constraints: a limit on total funding and on allowable expense types; quotas per event, per faculty mentor and per academic year; reputation of the event and organisers; compliance with pre-, during and post-event requirements.
  • Branding: state De La Salle University, the College of Computer Studies and the degree program on all materials, and show the official DLSU and CCS logos.
  • Within 7 working days after the event, submit a high-resolution social media graphic and a 300–500 word article (event details, summary of the work, key learnings, acknowledgment).
  • Not complying may delay reimbursement and may affect the faculty mentor's eligibility for future grants.

Suggested changes

1. Add the Conference Report as a required document

The form says the grantee must submit one, but it is not in your list of ten documents. I added it as document 11.

2. Funding source per expense line, plus one for the grant

The form has a funding source column on every expense row. The Dean also sets a funding source for the grant. I store both, so reports can show either.

3. Eligibility pre-check that advises, not blocks

Show "once per academic year", "first-time applicant" and "one student per paper" as checks for the adviser and staff to confirm. The system should not auto-reject on rules it cannot verify.

4. A separate "Return for correction" decision

Approvers get Approve, Return for correction and Reject. Return sends the file back to the student and keeps all earlier decisions. Reject needs a reason and ends the application.

5. Conference indexing field

Ask for the index (Scopus, ISI or ASEAN Citation) and a link or file as proof, since eligibility depends on it. Reviewers see it on the details page.

6. Auto-name uploads with the form's convention

The form defines LN_FN-CONF-DocType-yyyymmdd.pdf. The system can apply it on upload, so staff do not rename files.

7. In-system approvals replace the attached endorsement

The form asks for attached endorsements. With Approve buttons, the audit record becomes the endorsement. Please confirm that the Office of the Dean and the FAO accept this, and whether a PDF of the approval history should be attached to the FAO report.

8. Strict separation of duties

Dean: approves the grant and final amount. Administrative Assistant: verifies and adjusts the verified amount only. Secretary: records and monitors. The backend refuses any other combination, and nobody can act on their own application.

9. Routing table managed by the System Administrator

Each application stores its adviser, chair and associate dean at submission time. If someone leaves, an audited reassignment moves the file without editing history.

10. Reference number format

Proposed CCS-CG-YYYY-NNNN, counting per calendar year. Open question: do you prefer academic-year numbering?

11. Add an event type

The guidelines cover conferences, competitions, exhibits and public fora. The paper form only covers conferences. A "type of event" field lets the same system handle all four, and the form fields adjust later.

12. Structure the article and graphic

Your items 8 and 9 match the guidelines' article and social media graphic. The article upload gets a word count check (300–500) and the four required parts as a checklist. The graphic is checked for resolution.

13. A 7-working-day clock

After the event ends, the system computes the due date and sends reminders. A late file shows a flag to staff and the mentor. It does not block anything, because the guidelines say lateness may delay reimbursement, not forbid it.

14. Branding acknowledgement

A required checkbox at submission, and a staff check on the verification screen that the uploaded paper and poster show the university, college, degree program and both logos.

15. Quota panel for approvers

Show counts per event, per faculty mentor and per academic year beside each application. The guidelines give no quota numbers, so the limits are settings the administrator fills in.

Proposed stack (needs your confirmation)

I do not know your current hosting or framework, so this is a default to react to. Tell me what the existing CCS Portal uses and I will match it.

Application

TypeScript web app with a REST API (OpenAPI documented) and a separate frontend.

Data

PostgreSQL with migrations. Append-only audit table. Documents on private storage, served through authorised links.

Sign-in

Google OAuth limited to the DLSU domain. Roles come from our own table, never from the Google profile.

Notifications

Queue with an idempotency key per event, so one event sends one email and one in-app item.

Reports

Fill the FAO template from stored data (DOCX or PDF, to match the template). Keep each generated copy linked to the application.

Google Forms

Not recommended. Per-document uploads, role rules and audit need a real backend. Forms and Sheets stay as import or export only.

Questions to answer before Step 3

  1. Does the P30,000 or P15,000 limit apply to the total subsidy per application? I assumed yes, and the mockups show that.
  2. Who is the "Department Chair" when the adviser is from another department: the student's department or the adviser's?
  3. May the Secretary record the final amount instead of the Dean, and which amounts need the Dean?
  4. How long must documents and reports be retained, and what are the publication and privacy rules for the write-up and poster?
  5. Which file types and size limit should uploads accept? The mockup uses PDF, JPG and PNG up to 10 MB.
  6. Do the guidelines have more pages? The two I received give no funding limit figures, allowable expense types, quota numbers or reimbursement procedure.
  7. What are the quotas per event, per faculty mentor and per academic year?
  8. Who receives the post-event article and graphic? The guidelines say the Department or College office where funding was secured.

CCS Guidelines for Student Funding SupportConferences, competitions, exhibits and public fora
Summarised from the two pages of the guidelines I received. Those pages do not give funding amounts, allowable expenses or a reimbursement procedure. The amounts shown come from the application form and are labelled.

The College provides partial funding for students to take part in local and international conferences, competitions, exhibits, public fora and other reputable academic venues.

Eligibility guidelines

Funding is discretionary and subject to:

  • Limits on the total funding amount and the types of allowable expenses.
  • A quota on grant recipients per event, per faculty mentor and per academic year.
  • The reputation of the event and its organisers.
  • Compliance with all pre-, during and post-event requirements.

Not complying may delay reimbursement and may affect the faculty mentor's eligibility for future grants.

Amounts and criteria application form

  • Up to ₱30,000 international, ₱15,000 local, subject to availability of funds.
  • Once every academic year.
  • Paper in a Scopus, ISI or ASEAN Citation listed proceedings.
  • Priority to first-time applicants. One student co-author per paper.

Branding guidelines

  • State De La Salle University, the College of Computer Studies and the degree program on the paper, poster, video, presentation materials, program or event registry.
  • Show the official DLSU and CCS logos on slides, videos, posters, exhibit materials and booth signage.
  • Identify proponents and mentors as members of the CCS community.

Required after the event guidelines

Within 7 working days, submit to the Department or College office where funding was secured:

  • A high-resolution social media graphic, following CCS brand guidelines.
  • An article of 300–500 words for the CCS website or newsletter: event details, summary of the work, key learnings and an acknowledgment.

The form also requires a conference report and receipts for liquidation.

    Duplicate protection

    Each notification has a key made of the event, application and recipient, for example approval_required:CCS-CG-2026-0042:chair:step2. If the same event fires twice, the second is dropped. A genuinely new event, such as a resubmission, has a new key and sends again.

    The email and the in-app notice are created together from that one event.

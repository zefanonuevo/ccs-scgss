CCS Guidelines for Student Funding SupportConferences, competitions, exhibits and public fora
Summarised from the two pages of the guidelines I received. Those pages do not give funding amounts, allowable expenses or a reimbursement procedure. The amounts shown come from the application form and are labelled.

The College provides partial funding for students to take part in local and international conferences, competitions, exhibits, public fora and other reputable academic venues.

Eligibility guidelines

Funding is discretionary and subject to:

  • Limits on the total funding amount and the types of allowable expenses.
  • A quota on grant recipients per event, per faculty mentor and per academic year.
  • The reputation of the event and its organisers.
  • Compliance with all pre-, during and post-event requirements.

Not complying may delay reimbursement and may affect the faculty mentor's eligibility for future grants.

Amounts and criteria application form

  • Grant limits set by the University, by where the event takes place: NCR ₱6,000, Luzon (outside NCR) ₱8,000, Ilocos, Cagayan or Bicol ₱10,000, Visayas or Mindanao ₱12,000, international ₱15,000. Subject to availability of funds.
  • Once every academic year.
  • Paper in a Scopus, ISI or ASEAN Citation listed proceedings.
  • Priority to first-time applicants. One student co-author per paper.

Branding guidelines

  • State De La Salle University, the College of Computer Studies and the degree program on the paper, poster, video, presentation materials, program or event registry.
  • Show the official DLSU and CCS logos on slides, videos, posters, exhibit materials and booth signage.
  • Identify proponents and mentors as members of the CCS community.

Required after the event guidelines

The guidelines say within 7 working days, to the Department or College office where funding was secured. The system now asks for the post-activity report and reimbursement requirements within 15 days after the event (to be reconciled with the guidelines). The guidelines list:

  • A high-resolution social media graphic, following CCS brand guidelines.
  • An article of 300–500 words for the CCS website or newsletter: event details, summary of the work, key learnings and an acknowledgment.

The form also requires a conference report and receipts for liquidation.

Application procedure system

  1. Fill in the application form and attach one PDF with all requirements.
  2. The system routes it to your Thesis Adviser, Department Chair, Associate Dean and Dean, one after the other.
  3. After approval, attend the event, then submit the post-activity report as one PDF within 15 days. You will get reminders by email and in-app.
  4. The Administrative Assistant verifies the documents and the receipts, the reimbursement report goes to the FAO, and you are told when the RFP is recorded.

Reimbursement rules not in pages received

Allowable expenses, limits and deadlines are not stated in the pages I have. The system will show them here when supplied.

Application form

Five steps. Pick the academic year and term, your degree program and Thesis Adviser, and attach one PDF with all requirements.

Post-activity report

After the event, within 15 days: list your receipts (OR or invoice no., merchant, amount) and upload one consolidated PDF with the post-conference documents. The Administrative Assistant verifies it.

Application tracking

Every application, with its stage, who it is waiting on, and its funding source.

Verify post-activity documents

See the total grant first. Then tick each document found in the PDF or flag it to return, check each receipt (amount, funding source, OR or invoice no., merchant, remark), and verify or return. Once verified, download the reimbursement report.

Reimbursement report

Generated from the verified amounts, with the final approved amounts only. Download it, submit it to the FAO, then record the RFP number here.

Google Sheet and Google Drive records

All details encoded per application go to one Google Sheet row. All uploaded files go to one designated Drive folder, named with the reference number.

Notifications

In-app notices, and the matching email copies.

Approval and reimbursement workflow

Solid lines are the normal path and run automatically after each decision. Dashed lines return the file to the student with remarks. The dotted line ends the application. Earlier decisions and every file version are always kept.

Versions

What changed in each version of the system, how versions are numbered, and where each one came from. Newest first.

Numbering

Major (2.0): how people work changes, such as a new role or a new approval step. Minor (1.1): new features or a new look that leave existing steps alone. Patch (1.1.1): fixes only.

Version 3.0.0

20 Oct 2026 · Prototype

The feedback round on reimbursement, the Dean's approval and the FAO. Because how people work changes (receipts are itemised, the Dean confirms before signing, the Administrative Assistant records an RFP, the report deadline is 15 days with automatic reminders, and the Secretary role is gone), this is a major version.

Added

  • Receipts list in the post-activity report: expense, OR or invoice no., merchant and amount. The total cannot be above the approved grant.
  • Dean's confirmation step before the grant is recorded; funding source typed on each line; a signature can be drawn or uploaded.
  • RFP number when the report is filed at the FAO, with a notice to the student to check with the FAO after 5 to 10 working days.
  • Download Reimbursement Report once the amounts are verified.
  • Period filter (academic year and term) on the Dean, approver and staff dashboards; an RFP column in the dashboard, tracking table and Sheet.
  • Automatic reminders to students for the post-activity report, by email and in-app.
  • Return requirements: the Administrative Assistant flags which documents to clarify or resubmit.
  • Send to in Messages: the Administrative Assistant can clarify with the student, any signatory and the Dean.
  • Adviser who is also a signatory: endorse once, repeated step skipped; an alternate approver when the Dean is the adviser.
  • An adviser demo in the Simulation tab.

Changed

  • The proposed budget is blocked above ₱30,000 international or ₱15,000 local.
  • The student sees only the approved grant when reporting. Amounts are in pesos.
  • The Administrative Assistant sees the total grant first and can correct amounts, funding source, OR or invoice no. and merchant per item.
  • The reimbursement report shows only the final approved amounts.
  • The post-activity report is due 15 days after the event (was 7 working days).
  • A corrected report is saved as a new version (v2); the original stays on record.
  • "Accounting Office" is now "FAO".

  • "Documents verified" from the reimbursement report, and the claimed, rate and unused balance columns.
  • The Secretary role and account.

Known limits

  • Reminders run when the app is opened. The real build needs a daily job.
  • DocuSeal is a suggestion only; nothing is built.

Version 2.0.0

15 Oct 2026 · Prototype

A working simulation. One application now travels through every role, with real routing rules, notifications, a post-activity report, verification and a reimbursement report. Because how people work changes (a new routing order, a Dean e-signature and a new verification step), this is a major version.

Added

  • Routing engine: Thesis Adviser, Department Chair (by degree program), Associate Dean (set once), then Dean, automatic after each decision.
  • Endorse, Return with remarks, Reject. Return keeps earlier decisions; Reject needs a reason.
  • Dean approval with an approved amount and funding source per expense line, and a digital signature.
  • Comments and clarifications with file uploads, between the student and the approvers.
  • Document preview on the page for every approver.
  • Post-activity report in one PDF with actual expenses and a peso conversion rate.
  • Verification by the Administrative Assistant: tick each document, remark, approved amount and funding source per item.
  • Reimbursement report for the Accounting Office, printable.
  • Notifications by email and in-app, with reminders, and an Approval History for every update.
  • Google Sheet and Drive view, plus google/Code.gs to make them real.
  • Settings, Simulation with a full demo and security checks, and seed applications at every stage.

Changed

  • Department is now Degree Program (BSCS, BSIT, BSIS).
  • Academic year and term are form fields. References and file names follow 2627-Term1-001.
  • Students confirm that all requirements are in one PDF. A Thesis Adviser field and a Data Privacy consent were added.
  • The dashboards, tracking table, verification and report now read live data instead of fixed samples.
  • The Dean's final amount step was removed: the Dean sets the grant, and the Administrative Assistant sets the reimbursement.

Known limits

  • Data lives in one browser. Sign out and in to switch roles.
  • The reimbursement report is template v1 and still to be polished by the College.
  • The drawn signature is not a certified digital signature.

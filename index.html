CCS Guidelines for Student Funding SupportConferences, competitions, exhibits and public fora
Summarised from the two pages of the guidelines I received. Those pages do not give funding amounts, allowable expenses or a reimbursement procedure. The amounts shown come from the application form and are labelled.

The College provides partial funding for students to take part in local and international conferences, competitions, exhibits, public fora and other reputable academic venues.

Eligibility guidelines

Funding is discretionary and subject to:

  • Limits on the total funding amount and the types of allowable expenses.
  • A quota on grant recipients per event, per faculty mentor and per academic year.
  • The reputation of the event and its organisers.
  • Compliance with all pre-, during and post-event requirements.

Not complying may delay reimbursement and may affect the faculty mentor's eligibility for future grants.

Amounts and criteria application form

  • Up to ₱30,000 international, ₱15,000 local, subject to availability of funds.
  • Once every academic year.
  • Paper in a Scopus, ISI or ASEAN Citation listed proceedings.
  • Priority to first-time applicants. One student co-author per paper.

Branding guidelines

  • State De La Salle University, the College of Computer Studies and the degree program on the paper, poster, video, presentation materials, program or event registry.
  • Show the official DLSU and CCS logos on slides, videos, posters, exhibit materials and booth signage.
  • Identify proponents and mentors as members of the CCS community.

Required after the event guidelines

The guidelines say within 7 working days, to the Department or College office where funding was secured. The system now asks for the post-activity report and reimbursement requirements within 15 days after the event (to be reconciled with the guidelines). The guidelines list:

  • A high-resolution social media graphic, following CCS brand guidelines.
  • An article of 300–500 words for the CCS website or newsletter: event details, summary of the work, key learnings and an acknowledgment.

The form also requires a conference report and receipts for liquidation.

Application procedure system

  1. Fill in the application form and attach one PDF with all requirements.
  2. The system routes it to your Thesis Adviser, Department Chair, Associate Dean and Dean, one after the other.
  3. After approval, attend the event, then submit the post-activity report as one PDF within 15 days. You will get reminders by email and in-app.
  4. The Administrative Assistant verifies the documents and the receipts, the reimbursement report goes to the FAO, and you are told when the RFP is recorded.

Suggestions from checking these changes

DocuSeal for secured signing

DocuSeal is an open-source e-signature platform (GitHub). It can be self-hosted, has an API and webhooks, can embed the signing form in a page, and keeps an audit log with a signed PDF. That would replace the drawn or uploaded image, which is a convenience mark and not a certified signature. A fit for this system: when the Administrative Assistant verifies, the app creates a DocuSeal request from the reimbursement report, the Dean signs it there, and the signed PDF returns to the Drive folder. Before choosing: confirm the licence (AGPL for the open-source edition), whether webhooks and API access are in the edition you would use, where it would be hosted, and whether the Office of the Dean and the FAO accept it. Sources disagree on which features are paid, so check docuseal.com.

One deadline for everything after the event

The guidelines say the social media graphic and the article are due within 7 working days. Your note sets 15 days for the post-activity report and reimbursement requirements. The system now uses 15 calendar days from the last day of the event for the whole package. Please confirm calendar or working days, and whether the graphic and the article follow the same date.

Caps per expense type

Reimbursement is checked against the total grant only, as you asked. A student could therefore claim more airfare than the Dean approved for airfare, if per diem is lower. Option: show the Administrative Assistant a warning when an item type goes over the Dean's amount for that type, without blocking.

Duplicate receipts

Warn when the same OR or invoice number and merchant appear twice in one report, or in another application. It is cheap to add and catches common mistakes.

Foreign receipts

With the currency removed, the student converts to pesos. The Administrative Assistant can only check that against the proof in the PDF. If this proves hard, add an optional "original amount and currency" note per receipt, shown only to the Administrative Assistant.

After the RFP

The student is told to check with the FAO after 5 to 10 working days. An optional last step, "released by the FAO" with a date, entered by the Administrative Assistant, would end the process with a clear notice instead of a waiting period.

Late reports

After the due date the system keeps reminding every 3 days but still accepts the report. Decide whether late reports are accepted, accepted with a flag, or need the Dean's approval. A late flag on the dashboard and the report would help the FAO.

Official PDF copy

The downloaded report is a plain text PDF made in the browser. In the real build, generate it on the server from the College's template, with the Dean's signature (or the DocuSeal result), and save it to Drive.

Questions that came out of this round

  1. When the Thesis Adviser is the Chair or the Associate Dean, is it right that they endorse once and the repeated step is skipped? Or should the next person up take that step?
  2. When the Dean is the Thesis Adviser, who approves the grant? The system uses an "alternate approver" in Settings. Who should that be?
  3. Is the 15-day deadline in calendar days, counted from the last day of the event?
  4. Can the Administrative Assistant raise an item above what the student listed (for example, a misread receipt), as long as the total stays within the grant? The system allows it with a remark.
  5. Should "Peso conversion rate" stay in the list of post-conference documents now that students enter pesos?
  6. What is the full name of the FAO, and what does an RFP number look like? The field accepts any text up to 40 characters.
  7. Does the downloaded report need the Dean's signature image inside the PDF, or is the printed copy enough until DocuSeal or another tool is chosen?

Ideas I added

Reminders after 2 days

Staff can send reminders for anything that has waited more than 2 days. The real build would run this every morning and could escalate.

Eligibility advice, not blocking

If a student already has an application in the same academic year, the review step and the approvers see a note. The grant is available once every academic year, but only people can judge exceptions.

Cap checks

The Dean cannot approve more than the student projected, or more than the travel limit. The Administrative Assistant cannot reimburse more than the Dean approved, and must explain any line that differs from the claim.

Drafts and file versions

Students can save a draft. Replaced files stay on record as earlier versions, and the Drive view marks them.

Separation of duties, enforced

Nobody acts on their own application, nobody skips a step, and only the Administrative Assistant verifies. Refused attempts are written to the history. The Simulation tab lets you try them.

Routing table in Settings

The System Administrator sets the Associate Dean, Dean, the alternate approver and the chair per degree program. Each application keeps its own copy of the signatories, so history is never rewritten.

Printable report

The reimbursement report prints cleanly to PDF, with the Dean's signature and a block for the FAO to receive it with the RFP number.

Later: PDF merge helper

A small tool that combines the student's separate files into the single PDF, so students do not need another program. Not built yet.

Honest limits of this prototype

  • Data is stored in this browser only. To follow one application across roles, sign out and sign in as the next role in the same browser.
  • The sign-in is a fixed demo password. Real sign-in would use Google limited to the DLSU domain.
  • The Google script uses a shared token, which is not user-level security. A production build should check the signed-in Google user on every request.
  • The Dean's signature is a drawn image kept with the grant. It is a convenience mark, not a certified digital signature. Please confirm what the Office of the Dean accepts.
  • The 15-day deadline counts calendar days from the last day of the event. Reminders in this prototype run when someone opens the app; the real build needs a daily job.
  • The sample PDFs for seeded applications are generated placeholders.

  3. Should the Administrative Assistant also check the application when it is submitted ("validate submitted application"), or only the post-activity documents? Today only the post-activity documents are validated.
  4. Is the Associate Dean the same person for every program, so one setting is enough?
  5. Who is the Thesis Adviser signatory when the paper does not come from a thesis?
  6. Does the Office of the Dean accept the in-system endorsements and the drawn signature in place of the signed cover letter?
  7. May an approved reimbursement exceed the Dean's approved grant in special cases, and who approves that?
  8. How long must documents and reports be kept, and may the write-up and poster be published?
  9. What are the quotas per event, per faculty mentor and per academic year?

Version 3.0.0

20 Oct 2026 · Prototype · current

The feedback round on reimbursement, the Dean's approval and the FAO. Because how people work changes (receipts are itemised, the Dean confirms before signing, the Administrative Assistant records an RFP, the report deadline is 15 days with automatic reminders, and the Secretary role is gone), this is a major version.

Added

  • Receipts list in the post-activity report: expense, OR or invoice no., merchant and amount. The total cannot be above the approved grant.
  • Dean's confirmation step before the grant is recorded; funding source typed on each line; a signature can be drawn or uploaded.
  • RFP number when the report is filed at the FAO, with a notice to the student to check with the FAO after 5 to 10 working days.
  • Download Reimbursement Report once the amounts are verified.
  • Period filter (academic year and term) on the Dean, approver and staff dashboards; an RFP column in the dashboard, tracking table and Sheet.
  • Automatic reminders to students for the post-activity report, by email and in-app.
  • Return requirements: the Administrative Assistant flags which documents to clarify or resubmit.
  • Send to in Messages: the Administrative Assistant can clarify with the student, any signatory and the Dean.
  • Adviser who is also a signatory: endorse once, repeated step skipped; an alternate approver when the Dean is the adviser.
  • An adviser demo in the Simulation tab.

Changed

  • The proposed budget is blocked above ₱30,000 international or ₱15,000 local.
  • The student sees only the approved grant when reporting. Amounts are in pesos.
  • The Administrative Assistant sees the total grant first and can correct amounts, funding source, OR or invoice no. and merchant per item.
  • The reimbursement report shows only the final approved amounts.
  • The post-activity report is due 15 days after the event (was 7 working days).
  • A corrected report is saved as a new version (v2); the original stays on record.

  • The funding source from the Dean's final decision, the funding source list in Settings, and every funding dropdown.
  • "Documents verified" from the reimbursement report, and the claimed, rate and unused balance columns.

Known limits

  • The downloaded report is a plain text PDF; the printed copy carries the signature image.
  • Reminders run when the app is opened. The real build needs a daily job.

  • Each role opens on its own dashboard: student, approver or admin staff.

Changed

Version 1.1.0

15 Oct 2026 · Prototype · commit 8a2df7c

A visual refresh in the style of a macOS app, matching CCS Task Tracker 2.5. Every feature works as before.

Added

  • Dark theme and a theme switch in the header: System, Light or Dark. The choice is remembered in this browser.
  • Motion that answers a press: press feedback 160ms, toasts 240ms in and 160ms out. Tab switches do not animate.
  • With Reduce motion turned on, movement is replaced by a 120ms fade.

Changed

  • New look: neutral surfaces, one blue accent, a frosted toolbar and segmented tabs. The system font replaces the downloaded fonts.
  • On phones the header scrolls away and only the tab strip stays pinned.
  • The tab strip scrolls sideways when it does not fit.

Fixed

  • Text in the Workflow diagram no longer runs past the edge of its boxes.
  • Progress steps inside cards stack correctly, pills no longer stretch, and small tables are no longer clipped.
